Project 1

FitFix: A prototype closet app

In this project, I contributed to the design of FitFix, a sustainable clothing app for closet tracking, shopping, and outfit inspiration. I designed the shopping experience, including browsing, checkout, and payment pages in Figma. Skills learned: • Creation of a prototype in Figma (screens, components, and basic layouts) • Designing a user friendly interface, spacing, readability, and color schemes • Creation a checkout page for an app, including card information and shipping
